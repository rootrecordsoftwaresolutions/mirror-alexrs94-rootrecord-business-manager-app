Cloud sync

            

              When you’re signed in, the app reads your local SQLite file (under the business_data folder in Account → Data locations), queues changes in sync_outbox, and exchanges them with the RootRecord license API (/v1/sync/push and pull) — not from a table named business_data on the server.

Data locations

            
Your main data file:

            

            

              This app keeps its main database under …\RootRecord\Business Manager\business_data\ (plus account files next to that). Older installs may still have RootRecord\data or RootRecord\users for legacy paths; new data for Business Manager V2 is the path shown above.
            

            

          


          

            
Danger zone

            

              Deletes your main data file, tracking state, attachments under your profile, and any feedback waiting to send. The app creates fresh data the next time it loads.
              This cannot be undone. Copy your data folder elsewhere first if you need a backup.
              When you uninstall from Windows (Add or remove programs), the uninstaller asks whether to remove data under your profile as well.
            

            
Type the phrase DELETE ALL DATA in the box, then click the button.

            
            

            

            
Cloud sync queue

            

              Removes all synced change history stored for your account on RootRecord servers (the queue other devices merge from).
              Your database and files on this computer stay unchanged. Next sync may re-upload from this PC or merge from peers.
              This cannot be undone.
            

            

              Type the phrase DELETE CLOUD DATA in the box, then click the button.

About & Help

          


          

            

              RootRecord Business Manager is your local-first workspace on Windows for
              time and tracking, money and clients, schedule and bookings,
              stock and supplies, and (with Pro) reports with charts and exports.
              Your business database path appears in the footer when data is loaded; core workflows work offline. Sign-in connects subscription,
              sync, and optional online features when you use them.
            


            
What RootRecord stands for

            

              Your grounding root for productivity, operations, and real-world data. RootRecord is built to make complex environments easier to
              operate in one calm workspace.
            

            
Principles:

              notes and records, and community touchpoints—where each app focuses on a specific domain (this one: business on your PC).
            


            
Plans and billing

            

              Pro adds the Reports workspace (charts and tables), multiple businesses with optional combined rollups, and planned
              features such as optional cloud backup and AI-assisted reporting.
              Free keeps your data on this computer with one active business and all core tools except that Reports workspace.
              Checkout and plan changes will appear in the app when billing is available; until then, use Account Settings for your
              membership summary and Feedback (category Billing / account) or the contact email below for account questions.

Data safety

            

              Keep regular backups of your database and any export files you rely on. If you use sync or cloud-related features when available,
              treat them as a convenience layer—your authoritative copy is still the file on disk you control.
            


            
Privacy, account, and online services

            

              Your primary business database stays on this PC unless you export it or use optional features that send data over the internet.
              Sign-in uses encrypted HTTPS to RootRecord account services so we can validate your subscription and identity.
              Sync, when you use it, transmits queued work data tied to your session so it can merge with your account backup—only after you are signed in.
              Feedback may be stored locally until it can be sent, or delivered to a configured endpoint if your environment provides one.
            

            

              Installs from the Microsoft Store receive updates through the Store. Other packaged installs may check for application updates (for example from GitHub Releases published by RootRecord). You are prompted before download and before restart—updates replace the installed app binaries; they do not run arbitrary remote scripts inside the product.
              The authoritative description of what we collect and how we use it is our
              Privacy Policy
              on rootrecord.info (required for Microsoft Store listing and recommended reading before you sign in).
